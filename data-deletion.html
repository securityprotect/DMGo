Data Deletion Policy

Business: DMGo | Effective Date: May 12, 2026

This page explains how DMGo users can request deletion of account and associated data from our system.

1. How to Request Deletion

Send an email to 20meghagaur@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request. Please include your registered account details so we can verify ownership.

2. Verification Process

For account security, DMGo may request additional verification information before processing a deletion request.

3. Deletion Scope

  • Personal profile and account details are removed from active systems.
  • Operational records may be anonymized where full deletion is not immediately possible.
  • Some records may be retained for legal, regulatory, anti-fraud, or dispute-resolution purposes.

4. Processing Timeline

Verified requests are generally processed within 7 to 30 business days, depending on data complexity and legal obligations.

5. Third-Party Services

If your data was shared with integrated service providers for platform operations, DMGo will coordinate deletion requests with them where applicable.

6. Contact

For deletion status or questions, contact 20meghagaur@gmail.com.
